# 3.7 - The Profile Update

**Made By: ImPoPzzZ & Sa1monfr**

Endless now has a profile: two traits that change how your run plays. This update includes everything from 3.6.

---

## Your profile (Endless)

- On the Endless setup screen, press **Profile**. Pick **two of 34 traits** (Paranoid, Lone Wolf, Fearless, Methodical, Hypervigilant Behaviour, Persistent Dread and more). Point at a trait to read what it does, click to pick it, click again to drop it. **Clear** starts over.
- Every trait has **a bonus and a price**, so nothing is free. A careful trait might make holds quicker but slow you down; a dark one might warn you when Mama is near but ring false alarms.
- **Traits never earn extra shards.** They change how the run plays, not how much it pays. Difficulty is still the only shard dial.
- Your two picks give you a **title** (for example "The Methodical Survivor"), shown on the setup screen and in the corner of your run. Some pairs are **named combos** with a small extra of their own.
- Some traits add things you can see and hear: a heartbeat when Mama is close, a word on screen telling you which way she went, whispers, phantom Mamas that are not really there, a **skip key (X)** that skips one objective, and a different gadget every objective.
- Some pairs cannot go together (Lone Wolf and the attachment traits, for example). The screen tells you why.
- Traits are for Endless only: not Versus, not Papa's party, not Recalled. Pick none and Endless plays exactly as before.

## The profile screen

- Drawn as **Waygetter's file on you**: your title across the top, two slot cards, the steady traits in green and the conditions in orange, and a paper card that shows the **bonus and the price** of whatever you point at. Traits that cannot pair with your other pick are greyed out.

## The setup screen

- Taller, with a Profile row and a **Your profile** box that spells out your traits, their bonuses and their prices.

Everyone needs 3.7 to play online together (Versus, Air Hockey and Recalled).
