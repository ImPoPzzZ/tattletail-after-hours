# Tattletail: After Hours

**Made By: ImPoPzzZ & Sa1monfr**

A free mod for Tattletail that turns the game into an Arcade full of things to play, with Mama always somewhere in the dark.

Launch Tattletail and you load straight into the Arcade. Walk up to a machine, step through a door, or grab a friend.

**NEW IN 3.7 - THE PROFILE UPDATE.** Endless now has a profile: before a run you pick two traits (of 34), each with a bonus and a price, and they change how the run plays. Recalled's story, new look and mounts from 3.7 are in too.

---

## What's in it

**Recalled** - a third-person co-op game for up to four players. Five areas, five bosses (Patches, the Display Model, the Hall Monitor, Papa and Mama), 25 jobs from the kids, levels 1 to 15, a treehouse for a safe place, crafting, gear, perfect blocks and dodge rolls. Alone, with friends, or online.

**Endless** - objective after objective with Mama hunting you. Modifiers, a phone that rings, shards to earn, and now a profile of two traits that changes how you play.

**Versus** - one of you is the kid, the other is Mama (or Papa). Invite a friend through Steam.

**The Arcade** - your hub. Shop, gadgets, machines, a mirror.

**Pinball** - a real Tattletail pinball machine with a House that eats the ball.

**Air Hockey** - first to 7, against Tattletail or a friend.

**Summer Vacation** - a beach, a smoothie bar, a break from Mama.

**Characters** - the boy, the girl, or build your own in the Character Creator. Outfits to buy with shards. Free DLC characters: Dez, Kaz, the Rat King, **ImPoPzzZ the plague doctor** and **Sa1monfr, a very strong man with a fish's head**.

## How to install

You need Tattletail, from Steam or itch.io:
- Steam: https://store.steampowered.com/app/568090
- itch.io: https://waygetter-games.itch.io/tattletail

Then:
1. Download **Install Tattletail After Hours.exe** and run it. It finds Tattletail and installs or updates the mod in one click.
2. Launch Tattletail. You'll load straight into the Arcade.

Prefer to do it by hand? Download the zip, copy everything into your Tattletail folder (the one with tattletailWindows.exe in it), and choose "Replace the files in the destination" if Windows asks. The README inside the zip has every step.

**Updating** is the same thing. Your progress is safe: your shards, unlocks and saves live in Documents\Tattletail After Hours, and the zip doesn't touch them.

**Windows might say "Windows protected your PC"** the first time you run the installer, because it's new and unsigned. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway". Some antivirus programs also delete winhttp.dll, which is what starts the mod; if the menu looks normal, restore it from your antivirus history and add the Tattletail folder as an exclusion.

**Steam Deck / Linux:** set the launch option `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winhttp=n,b" %command%`
