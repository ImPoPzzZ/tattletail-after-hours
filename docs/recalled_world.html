The Town Where Recalled happens: the map, the zones, and the way through them

World 1 is one town at night, taken over by recalled toys. The treehouse sits in the middle. Five zones surround it, each harder than the last, ending at the Waygetter warehouse and Mama.

How the town works Proposed

Already decided and built into this layout: World 1 is the whole town, the hideout is a treehouse, the streets are lit and the side streets and buildings are dark, the level cap is 15, Papa is the junkyard's boss and Mama is the last one. Everything below is how I'd arrange that.

  • A wheel, with the treehouse at the hub. Every zone is a short walk from the park, so getting back to craft, stash and regroup is never a long trip, and friends at different points can meet in the middle.
  • You arrive on Main Street. The door in the Arcade opens onto the south end of Main Street. The Arcade is a building in the town. The treehouse is a one-minute walk north, and the suburbs are right beside you.
  • Lit means safer, dark means loot. Street lamps line the main roads between zones. Toys keep off them more. The good scrap, the quest objectives and the elites are in the dark: backyards, shop floors, corridors, basements.
  • A landmark for every zone. The water tower, the mall sign, the school's clock tower, the junkyard crane and the warehouse smokestack with its red light are visible from almost anywhere, so you can find your way without a minimap.
  • Level gates. Each zone's entrance is a barricade the kids built. It opens for you at the zone's level, once you've beaten the boss before it.
  • Forts and ziplines. Clearing a zone's first area lets the kids build a small fort there: a lit checkpoint where you can be revived to, and a zipline back to the treehouse and out again. It cuts the walking once a zone is familiar.
  • The edge. A quarantine fence with recall-notice tape surrounds the town. Beyond it is fog. One road leads out, and it's closed: that's where later worlds go.

The treehouse Decided: a treehouse

A huge old oak in the middle of the town park, with platforms built around the trunk. It's the only warm light in town. Toys can't climb it, and that's the whole reason the kids are still here. Everything about it below is proposed.

PlatformWhat's there
The ground, under the treeA rope ladder up, a ring of lanterns, the paths to the four streets. You arrive here from the zipline.
Lower deckThe crafting benches and the quest board. The kids who give quests sit here.
Middle deckYour stash (100 slots, safe when you die) and the trading table.
Zipline deckFour lines, one to each zone's fort. A line appears when its fort is built.
The lookoutThe top. You can see every landmark from here, and the warehouse's red light.

The park around it (about 90 m across) is open grass with a few benches, a dry fountain and a bandstand. Nothing spawns inside the park.

The zones Proposed

The suburbs levels 1–4 · about 200 m by 120 m

Three quiet streets of family houses, south-west of Main Street. Porch lights still on, toys left on lawns, a car with its door open. It teaches everything: light, noise, packs, your first elite, your first boss.

Areas
Maple Street (lit, the way in), the backyards (dark, fenced, loot), three houses you can enter, a garage row, the playground, the cul-de-sac under the water tower.
Enemies
Purple strays in packs of 3 to 4 on the lawns. Yellow ones in the backyards.
Elite
The jack-in-the-box: it hides in garages and boxes and springs when you're close.
Boss arena
The cul-de-sac. Patches: one huge Tattletail stitched together from many, in every color.
Materials
Springs, plastic, fur, screws.
Fort
The playground's climbing frame, fenced with planks.

The mall levels 4–7 · about 140 m by 145 m

East of the park. A parking lot of abandoned cars, then two floors of dead shops around an atrium. The first big indoor space: long sight lines in the atrium, tight dark corridors behind the shops.

Areas
The parking lot (lit by the mall sign), the atrium with its stopped escalators, the food court, the toy store, the service corridors (pitch dark), the loading bay.
Enemies
Teal Tattletails in the shops. Yellow ones in the lot.
Elite
RC cars: they patrol the parking lot and the atrium floor, and ram.
Boss arena
The toy store. The Display Model: the giant Tattletail from the shop window, three meters tall.
Materials
Batteries, circuit boards, plastic, the first voice boxes.
Fort
The security booth at the parking lot's entrance.

The school levels 7–10 · about 150 m by 140 m

West of the park. The place every kid knows, turned wrong. Almost all indoors and almost all dark: this is the scariest zone before the warehouse.

Areas
The yard (lit), the locker halls, classrooms, the cafeteria, the library, the gym, the boiler room in the basement.
Enemies
Black and White Tattletails. They hide in lockers and under desks, and stay silent longer.
Elite
The robot: it walks the halls on a route and shoots on sight. You can learn its route and slip past.
Boss arena
The gym. The Hall Monitor: a tall robot made from several, with a searchlight.
Materials
Circuit boards, eyes, voice boxes, screws.
Fort
The bus shelter in the yard.

The junkyard levels 10–13 · about 140 m by 135 m

North-east, across the rail tracks. A maze of stacked cars and scrap hills with no straight lines and almost no lamps. The best scrap in town is here, and so is Papa.

Areas
The rail crossing (the way in), the car stacks (a maze), the scrap hills, the crusher, the crane, Papa's den.
Enemies
Every color, in bigger packs. Gold Tattletails appear here as rare elites.
Elite
Fused toys: two or three of the earlier elites welded together.
Boss arena
The crusher pit, under the crane. Papa, on a throne of scrap.
Materials
All of them, in quantity, plus rare parts only found here.
Fort
The signal box at the rail crossing.

The Waygetter warehouse levels 13–15 · about 190 m by 85 m

North, at the end of Main Street, behind the last gate. Where the recalled toys were brought to be destroyed, and where they weren't. No fort and no zipline: you walk in from the gate every time.

Areas
The loading docks, the shelving canyon (aisles of boxed toys, three stories high), the conveyor hall, the recall line, the offices, Mama's chamber.
Enemies
The toughest of every color. Diamond Tattletails appear here as rare elites.
Before the fight
Mama hunts you through the building. You can't hurt her yet: you hear her, and you hide.
Boss arena
Mama's chamber, at the far end. Mama. Beating her at level 15 ends World 1.
Materials
The rarest parts, for the best gear in World 1.
Built from
The Waygetter Facility that already exists in the mod (three floors, decay and all) can be the inside of this building.

Building it Proposed

  • Built in code, like everything else. The town goes at its own height in the world, far from the house, the Arcade (y 400), the beach (y 300) and the factory (y 200). Positions come from marks placed with the E tool.
  • One zone at a time in memory. The whole town is too much to keep built at once on a 2016 engine. The park and the streets are always there; a zone's buildings, props and enemies are built when you approach its gate and removed when you're far away. Fog hides the distance.
  • Few real interiors. Most houses and shops are fronts. Each zone has a handful of buildings you can enter, and those are where the design effort goes.
  • The slice first. The first thing to build is the smallest playable piece of this map: the Arcade door, Main Street, the park and treehouse, and the suburbs up to Patches.
