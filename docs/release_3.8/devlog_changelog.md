# 3.8 - The Daily Update

**Made By: ImPoPzzZ & Sa1monfr**

Three new goals every day, one currency for everything, and Recalled gets its own music. This update includes everything from 3.7.

---

## Daily objectives

- Walk into the Arcade and you will see **three daily objectives** pinned by the mirror (just south of it), and listed in the **top right corner** of your screen the whole time you are in the Arcade.
- Every day brings **one easy, one medium and one hard** objective. They change at midnight, your time, and they are the same all day.
- Each one pays its **own reward in shards**: 25 for the easy one, 60 for the medium one, 120 for the hard one. Finish one and it is gone from the wall until tomorrow. Finish all three and the wall says so.
- The goals come from across the Arcade: finish objectives in Endless, survive for a while, escape on the phone, break vases and light candles, serve customers or play a day of Summer Vacation, survive Papa's party, defeat toys in Recalled, spend shards in the shops, earn shards.
- Not happy with the three you got? A **REROLL QUEST** paper sits under them. Hold on it to swap every objective you have not finished yet for another one of the same kind. **You can use it once a day.**
- If you finish one while you are in another game, the message is waiting for you when you are back in the Arcade.

## One currency: shards

- **Party Tokens and Versus Tokens are gone.** Everything pays **shards** now, and every shop takes shards.
- Any tokens you still had were turned into shards automatically.
- New payouts: **Papa's party** pays 30 shards for every full minute. **Versus** pays 150 for a catch and 30 for each of the child's objectives. **Pinball** pays up to 100 shards for a game. **Air Hockey** pays 40 for a win and 10 for a loss. **Recalled** pays 20 shards for every job handed in and 100 for every boss.
- The prices of the shops did not change.
- Summer Vacation still has its own Cash for Mama's upgrades (and its small shard bonus at the end of each day).

## Endless setup screen

- The Endless setup screen was cramped, so it has been re-laid out: your run settings and your look on the left, the run modifiers on the right, and **one details box** underneath that explains whatever you point at (a modifier, your gadget or your profile).

## Recalled

- Recalled now has **recorded music**: a background track for the town, a combat track for each of the five areas, and a track for the Waygetter Facility.
- The unfinished second world is out of the build for now.
