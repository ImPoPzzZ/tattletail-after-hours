## New: Recalled

The toys were recalled. They didn't stay recalled.

Recalled is a third-person co-op game set in a town at night that the Waygetter toys have taken over. Play it alone, with up to three friends, or online with other players.

**Getting there.** Hold to use the door in the Arcade, then pick one:
- **Go out alone**
- **Invite friends** - Steam's invite list opens right there
- **Go out online** - you're put in a town with other players near your own progress

**The town.** Five areas, each with a boss:
- The Suburbs - Patches
- The Mall - the Display Model
- The School - the Hall Monitor
- The Junkyard - Papa
- The Waygetter Facility - Mama

Tattletails are the enemies. Each area's barricade opens once you're the right level and you've beaten the boss before it. The mall and the school have a second floor, and the school has a boiler room underneath.

**The treehouse.** In the park, the one safe place. The kids live up there. Climb the ladder with W and S. Your crafting bench, the quest board and your stash chest are on its decks, and you mend fast.

**Jobs.** 21 of them, from the kids. Press **J** any time to see the one you're on with every step spelled out, and **M** for a map of the town. An arrow at the top of the screen always points the way you'd walk. When you hand in the first job in an area, the kids build a fort there with a zipline back to the treehouse.

**Levels.** Up to 15. Every level adds 30 health, and new gear needs a level to use.

### Fighting
- **Left click** attacks. Keep holding after a swing to wind up a heavy swing, or to draw back a slingshot or blaster. Let go to hit or fire.
- **Right click** (hold) blocks with your weapon. A blue bubble of light comes up. Press it just before a hit lands for a **perfect block**: no damage, the toy is thrown off balance, and your next blow counters for more.
- **Alt** rolls out of the way. You can't be hit for most of the roll.
- Every toy winds up before it hits, and an orange bar fills over its head while it does. Roll, block, or get out of the way.
- Running is loud, walking isn't, and toys hear your weapons too.
- **1 / 2 / Q** switch weapon. **F** is your flashlight.

### Gear and crafting
- Weapons, gadgets, armor and trinkets. Each armor point is 1 percent less damage from everything.
- Materials come from toys and from scrap piles (press E at a heap; it has more after 3 minutes).
- The bench uses your backpack and your stash together, and tells you exactly what an item does before you make it.
- Boss parts make your backpack bigger.
- If you go down, a friend can help you up. If nobody does, you wake up in the treehouse and the materials you were carrying stay where you fell.

### With friends
- **P** opens the friends screen. **G** marks what you're looking at. **5 6 7 8** say something. **GIVE** in the backpack hands an item to the friend beside you.
- Everyone keeps their own character, loot and job. A boss one of you has beaten is still waiting for whoever hasn't.
- Toys get tougher the more of you there are.

### Saves
Recalled saves to its own protected file in Documents\Tattletail After Hours, tied to your Steam account. Your other saves are unchanged.
