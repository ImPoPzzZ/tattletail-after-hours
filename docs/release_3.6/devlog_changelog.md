# 3.6 - The Story Update

**Made By: ImPoPzzZ & Sa1monfr**

Recalled gets its story, a new look, and something to ride. This update includes everything from 3.5.

---

## Recalled: the story

- **The kids talk.** Every job has words before and after it. They also have something to say when you get back to the treehouse, after a boss, when you level up and when you've been knocked out. It all shows in a text box at the bottom of the screen, with the speaker's name on a tab.
- **Out in the town**: Waygetter's announcements over the speakers, the toys muttering the Tape, the bosses, and a sign at every fort.
- **15 Recall Notices.** Waygetter's own papers, three in every area, lying on top of scrap piles. Press **E** to read one. They are collected under **NOTICES** on the J screen.
- **The end of the first world.** There are 25 jobs now, up from 21. When Mama falls, her Tape is in her back. Take it to the treehouse, listen to it with Moss, and decide what to do about what you hear. Then the Recall Express is waiting at the junkyard station, east of the park.
- **Last Stop** is where the train goes next. The board already lists it. It is under development.

## Recalled: a new look

- Every menu has a wooden frame with a gold edge, a title banner and plated buttons that light up.
- A night sky with stars, a faint Milky Way and the moon. Dust, spores, haze and embers drift differently in each area, there are fireflies in the park, and every area has its own colors.
- The town is dressed: mailboxes, hydrants, bins, cones, puddles, tricycles, shopping carts, a school bus, delivery trucks, swings, clotheslines, string lights and more. Houses have roofs, shutters and porch lights, and recalled Tattletails wait in parcels on the porches.
- Better lighting: soft shadows in corners, a glow on shiny things, moon shadows, and shafts of light through the fog from the lamps nearest you.
- If it costs you frames, turn it off with `Cosmetics / RecalledGraphics` in the config file.

## Recalled: mounts

- Three things to ride, made at the crafting bench: a **Toy Wagon** (from the start), a **Ride-On RC Car** (after Patches) and a **Rocking Horse** (after the Hall Monitor).
- Press **V** to get on or off. You're 55 percent faster, you can't fight, and a hit throws you off.
- Seven RC cars wait in the mall's parking lot, and one comes back 40 seconds after it's broken.

## Fixes

- The Arcade could hang on its loading screen when the game refused to load the level. It now waits and asks again.
- Recalled shows its own loading screen all the way until the town is built, instead of a frozen picture of the Arcade.
- Words in the text boxes wrap inside the box.
- Co-op: everyone in a town works on the same job, and your own progress is kept safe. A better save is never replaced by a worse one.

Everyone needs 3.6 to play online together (Versus, Air Hockey and Recalled).
